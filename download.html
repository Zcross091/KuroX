Quick Start

Installation Guides

📱

Android & Android TV

1. Tap the download button above to save the APK directly.
2. When opening, tap "Settings" and toggle "Allow from this source" if prompted.
3. Tap Install. Future updates install cleanly without deleting old versions thanks to our permanent release keystore.

🪟

Windows 10 & 11

1. Download KuroX-windows-setup.exe.
2. If Windows SmartScreen displays "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and select "Run anyway".
3. Follow the quick setup wizard. KuroX will automatically register with Discord for Rich Presence!

🐧

Linux Desktop

1. Extract the tarball: tar -xzf KuroX-v2.1.12-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
2. Navigate to directory: cd bundle
3. Grant execution rights and launch: chmod +x shonenx && ./shonenx

🍏

macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel)

1. Download KuroX-macos-universal.zip and extract it.
2. Drag KuroX.app into your /Applications folder.
3. Right-click KuroX.app and click Open (or run xattr -cr /Applications/KuroX.app in Terminal to bypass Gatekeeper).

📱

iOS & iPadOS (Sideloading)

1. Download KuroX-ios-unsigned.ipa to your iPhone/iPad or computer.
2. Open AltStore, Sideloadly, or SideStore and select the downloaded IPA.
3. Sign in with your free Apple ID to sign and install. On your iPhone, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and tap "Trust".
